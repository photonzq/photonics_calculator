Cavity Resonators & Guided Waves

⭕ Microresonator Loss & Linewidth

$$ \mathcal{F} = \frac{\text{FSR}}{\kappa_0/2\pi} \qquad Q = \frac{f_0}{\kappa_0/2\pi} \qquad \alpha = \frac{n_g \kappa_0}{c} \qquad \text{Loss/rt} = 1 - e^{-\alpha L_{\text{rt}}} \approx \frac{2\pi}{\mathcal{F}} $$

🪞 Fabry-Pérot (FP) Cavity

$$ \mathcal{F} = \frac{\pi \sqrt{r_{\text{rt}}}}{1 - r_{\text{rt}}}, \quad Q = \frac{2 n_g L}{\lambda_0}\mathcal{F}, \quad r_{\text{rt}} = \sqrt{R_1 R_2}\,e^{-\alpha L} $$
